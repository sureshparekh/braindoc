The science of the fit#

BRAIN describes a galaxy’s spectrum as the light of a mixture of simple stellar populations, attenuated by dust, shifted and broadened by the motions of the stars, with emission lines from the ionised gas and, in nespop, the nebular continuum of the young stars’ ionising photons. It finds the mixture that reproduces the spectrum pixel by pixel, and reports what the mixture implies, with an uncertainty for each quantity.

These pages state exactly what is fitted, and how. They are written for a reader who wants to know what a number means before quoting it.

Conventions#

Throughout, wavelengths are in Å and in the rest frame unless stated, velocities in km s⁻¹, extinctions in magnitudes; \(\lambda_0\) is the normalisation wavelength (5500 Å by default) and \(k(\lambda) = A_\lambda/A_V\) the extinction law. Light fractions refer to \(\lambda_0\). The fit’s sequence follows FADO (Gomes & Papaderos 2017) where the two codes do the same thing, and the text says where they differ.
