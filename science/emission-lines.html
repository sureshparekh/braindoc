Emission lines and resolution#

In espop and nespop every line of the catalogue that can reach the fitted range is measured. The strong lines are columns of the linear system, each with a free non-negative amplitude, and their velocity and dispersion are non-linear parameters shared with the other lines of their kinematic group.

The line profile#

Each line is a Gaussian in \(\ln\lambda\), centred at \(\lambda_{\rm rest}(1+v_g/c)\), integrated over each data pixel between that pixel’s own edges in closed form:

\[ \phi_i(\lambda_c; v, \sigma) = \frac{1}{\Delta\lambda_i}\int_{\ln\lambda_i^-}^{\ln\lambda_i^+} \frac{c}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sigma}\, \exp\!\left[-\frac{c^2(u - \ln\lambda_c - v/c)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right] du , \]

with the width \(\sigma = (\sigma_g^2 + \sigma_{\rm inst}^2(\lambda_c))^{1/2}\): the gas’s own dispersion and the instrument’s, in quadrature. The profile has unit area at any width, even narrower than a pixel; a Gaussian sampled at pixel centres would lose most of a narrow line’s flux and impose a floor on \(\sigma_g\). The line is attenuated by the stars’ \(r_\lambda(A_V)\) (Dust), so its fitted weight is its flux before that dust.

The catalogue#

The lines are stored once, at vacuum wavelengths from NIST (H\(_2\) from Roueff et al. 2019); air wavelengths are derived from them with Ciddor (1996). Which set is used is wavelength_scale, a property of the observed spectrum: air and vacuum differ by about 83 km s⁻¹ across the optical, and a wrong choice moves every gas velocity by that much with no other symptom, so the answer is required rather than guessed. The catalogue holds 70 optical and 28 near-infrared lines: the Balmer series to H16, [O II], [Ne III], [O III] (4363, 4959, 5007), He I, He II, [O I], [N II], [S II], [S III], [Fe III], [Fe VII], [Ar IV] and others in the optical, and Paschen, Brackett, [S III], He I 10830, H\(_2\) and [Fe II] in the near-infrared.

A line that cannot land on the fitted range at any velocity and dispersion inside the bounds is left out, and named in the log, rather than fitted as a column of zeros, so a flux of zero in the results is a real non-detection.

Which lines enter the joint fit#

The joint fit of stars and lines (step 2 of the sequence, The model) sets the gas kinematics and carries only the strong optical lines: the Balmer series to H10, [O II], [Ne III], [O III], [O I], [N II] and [S II], and every line beyond 7500 Å. The auroral and trans-auroral lines ([O III] 4363, [N II] 5755, [S II] 4068 and 4076, [O II] 7320 and 7330) and the other faint lines are measured afterwards, on the data minus the continuum, at their group’s kinematics: with a free amplitude in the joint fit, a faint line sitting on a stellar feature takes flux from the template mismatch and widens its group.

Kinematic groups#

The lines of a group share \((v_g, \sigma_g)\). By default the hydrogen, low-ionisation and near-infrared lines (H\(\alpha\), H\(\beta\), the Balmer and Paschen series, [N II], [S II], [O I], [O II], He I, [Fe II], H\(_2\), …) form group 0, and the high-ionisation lines ([O III], [Ne III], He II, [Ar IV], [Fe VII], [Ne V], …) group 1. line_groups sets them explicitly:

line_groups:
  0: [Halpha, Hbeta, "[N II]", "[S II]"]
  1: ["[O III]", "[Ne III]"]

A group none of whose lines carries flux is reported as unconstrained rather than with the numbers the optimiser happened to stop at.

Several components per line#

In espop, line_components fits up to max_line_components Gaussian components per line, for outflows and other broad wings. A component is kept only when it lowers the Bayesian information criterion by more than component_bic_threshold and its strongest line reaches component_min_snr; the summary lists each step, and a component \(k\) of a line is named "<line> #k", narrowest first.

The instrument’s resolution#

A spectrograph smears every line by its line-spread function, and so did whatever produced the template library. The two are almost never equal, and their difference goes straight onto the dispersions BRAIN reports.

instrumental_resolution: 2.9             # a FWHM in Angstrom
instrumental_resolution: "70 km/s"       # constant in velocity, a 1-sigma width
instrumental_resolution: lsf.txt         # a file: wavelength, FWHM (Angstrom)

A string must carry its unit: "2.51" is refused rather than guessed, because Å and km s⁻¹ differ by a factor of about twenty. A plain number is a FWHM in Å; "45 km/s" is a 1σ width. A MaNGA cube’s own line-spread function is read from the cube.

What BRAIN does with them. For the stars, it convolves the templates up to the data’s resolution, once, before the fit, on each template’s own grid; it never smooths the data, since smoothing would correlate the noise. The decision is taken separately for each group of templates that shares a stated resolution, over the pixels actually fitted. For the lines, it adds \(\sigma_{\rm inst}(\lambda)\) to each line’s width in quadrature, as above, so the gas dispersion it reports is the gas’s own. The instrument acts at the observed wavelength \(\lambda_{\rm rest}(1+z)\), which is why the redshift that was removed matters even for a spectrum at rest.

In the results.

Convention

the signed quadrature difference of the two line-spread functions, data minus library

sigma_gas, megacube Sigma_gas_g*

intrinsic, the instrument being in the line model, when instrumental_resolution is given; sigma_inst is printed beside it

Every dispersion in the summary states its convention. If any template states no resolution and library_resolution is not set, no correction is applied and the intrinsic stellar dispersion is written as unknown rather than averaged over the templates that do state one. A sigma_star written with a leading < is an upper limit: the fit sat on the lower bound of dispersion_range, or the difference of the line-spread functions alone exceeds what the fit applied.

Degrading the data instead. A datacube can also be smoothed to a coarser resolution (degrade_resolution), to reproduce an older analysis. It correlates the noise and warns each time it runs, and asking for it together with instrumental_resolution is refused, since that would count the instrument twice.
