The nebular emission#

Young stars emit ultraviolet photons that ionise the gas around them, and the gas glows back: in emission lines, and in a smooth nebular continuum of free–free, free–bound and two-photon emission. That continuum is not flat. It has a Balmer jump at 3646 Å and it rises towards the red, so where it is present and not modelled, the fit absorbs it into the only quantities it has, the dust and the star formation history. nespop models it.

The nebular continuum of each population#

As in FADO (Gomes & Papaderos 2017), every stellar population carries the nebular continuum of the gas its own Lyman-continuum photons keep ionised. Column \(j\) of the stellar block gains

\[ q_j\, u_j\, N_\lambda, \qquad u_j = 10^{\,0.4\,(A_V + Y_V\delta_j)\,k(\lambda_0)}, \]

where \(q_j\) is the rate of hydrogen-ionising photons of template \(j\) per unit weight, integrated from the library’s own spectrum below 911.76 Å; \(u_j\) takes the weight back to the intrinsic flux at \(\lambda_0\); and

\[ N_\lambda = \big[\Gamma \ast \mathcal G(v_n, \sigma_n)\big]_\lambda\, 10^{-0.4\,A_V^{\rm neb}k(\lambda)}, \qquad \Gamma_\lambda = (1 - f_{\rm esc})\,\frac{c\,\gamma_{\rm eff}(\lambda; T_e, n_e)}{\lambda^2\,\alpha_B(T_e)}, \]

is the nebular continuum per ionising photon (Case B, every photon absorbed except the escaping fraction lyman_escape_fraction), broadened with the kinematics of the Balmer lines and reddened by the gas’s own extinction \(A_V^{\rm neb}\). The emission coefficient

\[ \gamma_{\rm eff} = \gamma_{\rm ff} + \gamma_{\rm fb,H\,I} + y_{\rm He}\,\gamma_{\rm fb,He\,I} + \gamma_{2q,\rm H\,I} + y_{\rm He}\,\gamma_{2q,\rm He\,I} \]

holds free–free, free–bound and two-photon emission of hydrogen and helium (\(y_{\rm He}\), helium_abundance, 0.25 by default). The term has no free amplitude: its strength follows from the populations the fit chooses, and since it is linear in the weights, the non-negative least squares solves for it exactly, with no iteration.

The fit, step by step#

  1. The espop fit measures the lines (steps 1–2 of The model).

  2. The gas conditions. \(T_e\) comes from [O III] (4959 + 5007)/4363 and \(n_e\) from [S II] 6716/6731, each where its lines reach S/N 3, on fluxes corrected by the gas’s own extinction; uncorrected, 4363 is reddened 0.22 \(A_V\) more than 5007, which put \(T_e\) about 3 per cent low on mocks. Where the lines are too faint, gas_temperature (10 000 K) and gas_density (100 cm⁻³) are used. A datacube’s spaxels are grouped in bins of 1000 K × 0.5 dex, each with its own nebular model.

  3. \(A_V^{\rm neb}\) from the Balmer decrement, against Case B at the measured \(T_e\), wherever H\(\alpha\) and H\(\beta\) are both at S/N ≥ 3, clipped to nebular_dust_range:

    \[ A_V^{\rm neb} = \frac{2.5}{k_{\rm H\beta} - k_{\rm H\alpha}} \log_{10}\frac{F_{\rm H\alpha}/F_{\rm H\beta}}{R_0(T_e, n_e)} . \]

    It is tied to the stellar \(A_V\) instead where H\(\alpha\) or H\(\beta\) is undetected, and where the decrement lies below anything Case B can give (H\(\alpha\)/H\(\beta\) below 2.75, the Case B value at 20 000 K, by more than 3σ) on an H\(\beta\) weaker than the stars’ own H\(\beta\) absorption (EW below 10 Å): that is Balmer absorption the stellar model missed, not gas.

  4. The populations with the nebular continuum, on the line-free pixels, \(\sigma_\star\) and \(v_\star\) free again. Where the spectrum qualifies (FC, below), two rows tie the Case B H\(\alpha\) and H\(\beta\) of the populations’ photons to the measured fluxes:

    \[ s_L\Big(C_L \sum_j q_j u_j w_j - F_L\Big) = 0, \qquad C_L = (1 - f_{\rm esc})\,\frac{4\pi j_L(T_e, n_e)}{n_e n_p\,\alpha_B(T_e)}\,10^{-0.4A_V^{\rm neb}k(\lambda_L)}, \]

    with \(C_L\) the Case B flux per ionising photon in line \(L\) (Storey & Hummer 1995) and the row weight \(s_L = \sqrt{\eta\,\max(1, \chi^2_\nu)}/F_L\), \(\eta\) being sc_penalty_weight. The rows are linear in the weights, so there is still no iteration, and scaling \(s_L\) by the reduced \(\chi^2\) makes the tie pull as hard at S/N 200 as at S/N 10.

  5. Every line measured on the data minus the stars and the nebular continuum, at the espop gas kinematics.

FC and NC#

The line tie applies only where the gas is ionised by stars and the lines can be trusted, as in FADO’s full-consistency (FC) mode: H\(\alpha\), H\(\beta\), [O III] 5007 and [N II] 6583 at S/N ≥ 3; star-forming or composite on the [N II] BPT diagram (below Kewley et al. 2001); a physical Balmer decrement; and [S II] 6716/6731 within 2σ of 0.40–1.50. Every other spectrum gets the nebular continuum with no line tie (NC). The summary’s treatment and the MODE plane of a megacube’s NEBMAPS say which each received.

The library must reach the ionising ultraviolet#

\(Q(\mathrm H)\) is integrated from each template shortward of 911.76 Å. A library that starts in the optical, such as BasesGM, has nothing to integrate, and BRAIN refuses nespop with it rather than fit a nebular component that would be identically zero. BC03 is the library nespop is validated with; M13 reaches the Lyman limit too, and runs with a warning that its ionising photons are untested (Template libraries). The continuum’s most distinctive feature is the Balmer jump at 3646 Å: a fit that starts redward of it constrains the continuum only through its smooth rise to the red, which is partly degenerate with dust.

How well it works#

On 144 mocks built with an independent code (a PyNeb Case B nebular continuum and lines, BC03 populations of 2 Myr, 300 Myr and 10 Gyr, a nebular continuum of 4 to 27 per cent of the light at 5500 Å, S/N 5–50), nespop recovered the nebular fraction to ±0.3 percentage points at every S/N (a median error of 0.04), H\(\beta\) to ±0.3 per cent at S/N ≥ 10, \(A_V^{\rm neb}\) to ±0.03 mag, and \(T_e\) to 1 per cent at S/N ≥ 20, whatever the extinction. FADO’s FC mode on the same mocks gave −1.5 to +0.6 points, ±0.4 per cent and ±0.07 mag.

On the spaxels of MaNGA 8626-12704 with S/N ≥ 10, BRAIN and FADO agree on \(y_{\rm neb}\) (rank correlation 0.96) and H\(\alpha\)/H\(\beta\) (1.00). \(A_V^{\rm neb}\) differs by +0.08 mag, because FADO assumes H\(\alpha\)/H\(\beta\) = 2.86 at any \(T_e\); \(T_e\) correlates at 0.84, BRAIN’s being 560 K higher, because FADO measures it on reddened fluxes.

Its limits#

  • Continuum S/N. \(A_V\) and the stellar ages need a continuum S/N of about 8–10. The FC nebular fraction does not, because the Balmer lines carry it.

  • FC needs the lines. Spectra that fail the tests get the nebular continuum alone, which is unreliable below a continuum S/N of about 8.

  • One Gaussian per line. Outflow wings are not fitted in nespop. Where a quarter of each line’s flux sits in a broad wing, H\(\beta\) comes out 8–10 per cent low and the FC nebular continuum follows it.

  • The resolution matters. Give the real line-spread function: it sets the relative widths of a group’s lines and so the Balmer decrement. Assuming a constant 2.75 Å instead of MaNGA’s own moved \(A_V^{\rm neb}\) by +0.07 mag.

  • \(T_e\) is measured only where [O III] 4363 reaches S/N 3.
