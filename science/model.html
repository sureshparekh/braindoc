The model#

The model of a spectrum#

BRAIN fits the normalised spectrum \(f_i = F_i / F_{\lambda_0}\) of every data pixel \(i\), where \(F_{\lambda_0}\) is the observed flux at the normalisation wavelength. The data are never resampled. The model is

\[\begin{split} \begin{aligned} M_i(\boldsymbol\theta, \mathbf w) ={}& \Big[\mathcal P\Big(\sum_{j\in\rm SSP} w_j \big\{\big[(b_j \circledast K_{\rm inst}) \ast \mathcal G(v_\star, \sigma_\star)\big]_\lambda\, r_\lambda(A_V + Y_V\delta_j) + q_j\, u_j\, N_\lambda\big\} \\ &\qquad + \sum_{a\in\rm FC} w_a\, \big[P_a \ast \mathcal G(v_\star, \sigma_\star)\big]_\lambda\, r_\lambda(A_V)\Big)\Big]_i \\ &+ \sum_{k=1}^{N_{\rm gas}} w^{\rm gas}_k \sum_{c\in k} \rho_{kc}\, \phi_i\Big(\lambda_{kc};\, v_{g(k)},\, \sqrt{\sigma^2_{g(k)} + \sigma^2_{\rm inst}(\lambda_{kc})}\Big)\, r_i(A_V) . \end{aligned} \end{split}\]

It has four parts.

The stellar populations. Each simple stellar population (SSP) template \(b_j\), normalised to 1 at \(\lambda_0\), is optionally convolved with a kernel \(K_{\rm inst}\) that brings the library’s resolution to the instrument’s (Emission lines and resolution), broadened and shifted by a Gaussian line-of-sight velocity distribution \(\mathcal G(v_\star, \sigma_\star)\), and attenuated by dust, \(r_\lambda(A) = 10^{-0.4A[k(\lambda)-k(\lambda_0)]}\), with the extra extinction \(Y_V\) for the templates flagged young (\(\delta_j = 1\)) (Dust).

The featureless continua. The AGN templates \(P_a\) (power laws, Cloudy AGN continua, blackbodies) when include_power_law is set: broadened and attenuated like the stars, carrying light but no mass.

The nebular continuum (nespop only). Each population carries the nebular continuum \(N_\lambda\) of the gas its own Lyman-continuum photons \(q_j\) keep ionised, scaled by \(u_j\), which takes the weight back to the intrinsic flux at \(\lambda_0\). The term has no free amplitude: it is part of the population’s column (The nebular emission).

The emission lines (espop, nespop). Each line \(k\) is a sum of members \(c\) with fixed ratios \(\rho_{kc}\) (a tied doublet), each a Gaussian integrated over the data pixel, \(\phi_i\), centred at \(\lambda_{kc}(1+v_g/c)\) with a width that adds the gas’s own dispersion and the instrument’s in quadrature. Lines share their kinematics within a group \(g(k)\) (Emission lines and resolution).

The stars and the nebular continuum are built on a fine grid uniform in \(\ln\lambda\), where a velocity distribution is a convolution done by FFT, and carried into the data’s pixels by the fixed, banded, flux-conserving operator \(\mathcal P\). The lines need no grid: each is integrated over the data pixel’s edges in closed form.

What is minimised#

\[ \chi^2(\boldsymbol\theta, \mathbf w) = \sum_i m_i \left[\frac{f_i - M_i(\boldsymbol\theta, \mathbf w)}{\sigma_i / F_{\lambda_0}}\right]^2 + \lVert D\,\mathbf w_\star\rVert^2 + \sum_{L\in\{\rm H\alpha, H\beta\}} s_L^2 \Big[C_L \sum_j q_j u_j w_j - F_L\Big]^2 , \]

with \(m_i\) the mask weight of pixel \(i\), \(D\) the optional smoothness operator on the stellar weights (The linear solve), and, in nespop only, two rows that tie the Case B H\(\alpha\) and H\(\beta\) of the populations’ photons to the measured fluxes \(F_L\) (The nebular emission). The solution is

\[ \hat{\boldsymbol\theta} = \arg\min_{\boldsymbol\theta \in [\boldsymbol\theta_{\rm lo}, \boldsymbol\theta_{\rm hi}]}\; \min_{\mathbf w \ge 0}\; \chi^2(\boldsymbol\theta, \mathbf w), \qquad \boldsymbol\theta = (A_V, Y_V, \sigma_\star, v_\star, \sigma_{g_1}, v_{g_1}, \dots) . \]

The weights \(\mathbf w\) enter linearly and are found exactly, by non-negative least squares, at every trial \(\boldsymbol\theta\) (The linear solve); the non-linear parameters \(\boldsymbol\theta\) by a projected Levenberg–Marquardt method with an exact Jacobian (Kinematics and the non-linear fit). One clip-and-refit pass at 3 times the larger of the error and the typical residual removes pixels the flags missed.

The fit is a sequence#

In the line modes, the fit follows FADO’s order (stellar_kinematics and population_fit both masked, the defaults):

  1. The stars alone, every line masked over ±1500 km s⁻¹ (kinematics_mask_width): \(\sigma_\star\) and \(v_\star\), which no gas line can pull.

  2. The stars and the strong lines together, \(\sigma_\star\) and \(v_\star\) held: the gas kinematics \((\sigma_g, v_g)\), and which lines are detected.

  3. nespop only: \(A_V^{\rm neb}\), \(T_e\) and \(n_e\) from the lines of step 2, and the choice between FC and NC.

  4. The populations on the pixels free of every detected line, \(\sigma_\star\) and \(v_\star\) free again, with the nebular continuum and the Case B rows in nespop: \(A_V\), \(Y_V\), \(\sigma_\star\), \(v_\star\) and the stellar weights.

  5. Every line, the auroral and faint ones included, by non-negative least squares on the data minus the continuum, at the kinematics of step 2: the line fluxes.

In spop only the population fit runs, over every unmasked pixel.

The symbols#

Symbol

Meaning

\(F_i\), \(\sigma_i\)

observed flux and error in data pixel \(i\)

\(b_j\)

SSP template \(j\), 1 at \(\lambda_0\)

\(P_a\)

featureless template: power law, AGN continuum or blackbody

\(w_j\), \(w_a\), \(w^{\rm gas}_k\)

non-negative weights; \(x_j = w_j/\sum w\)

\(K_{\rm inst}\)

optional kernel matching the library to the instrument

\(\mathcal G(v, \sigma)\)

Gaussian line-of-sight velocity distribution; \(\ast\) is convolution in \(\ln\lambda\)

\(\mathcal P\)

the fine log grid → data pixels operator

\(A_V\), \(Y_V\), \(\delta_j\)

extinction; extra extinction of young templates; 1 for a young template

\(k(\lambda)\)

the extinction law, \(A_\lambda/A_V\)

\(\phi_i\)

a unit-area Gaussian line integrated over pixel \(i\)

\(\sigma_{\rm inst}(\lambda)\)

the instrumental dispersion

\(\lambda_{kc}\), \(\rho_{kc}\)

rest wavelength and fixed ratio of member \(c\) of line \(k\)

\(q_j\), \(u_j\)

ionising photons of template \(j\) per unit weight; weight → intrinsic flux at \(\lambda_0\)

\(N_\lambda\)

the nebular continuum per ionising photon, broadened and reddened

\(C_L\), \(F_L\), \(s_L\)

Case B flux per photon in line \(L\); its measured flux; the tie’s weight

\(m_i\), \(D\)

the mask weight; the smoothness operator
