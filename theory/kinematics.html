Kinematics#

Log-wavelength sampling#

On a grid uniform in \(\ln\lambda\) a Doppler shift is a translation: \(\lambda_{\rm obs} = \lambda (1 + v/c)\) gives \(\ln\lambda_{\rm obs} \simeq \ln\lambda + v/c\), a shift of \(v / \Delta v\) pixels for every wavelength, and a velocity distribution becomes a convolution kernel independent of wavelength. The step \(\Delta v\) (velscale) is chosen from the data’s own sampling so that no information is thrown away.

Broadening in Fourier space#

For a Gaussian line-of-sight velocity distribution of mean \(v_\star\) and dispersion \(\sigma_\star\), each template is broadened and shifted at once in Fourier space:

\[ \tilde b_j(\omega) \;\longmapsto\; \tilde b_j(\omega)\, \exp\!\left[-\tfrac12\left(\omega \frac{\sigma_\star}{\Delta v}\right)^2 - i\,\omega \frac{v_\star}{\Delta v}\right], \]

with \(\omega\) the angular frequency in radians per pixel. The transform of every template is computed once per fit; a trial \((v_\star, \sigma_\star)\) costs one kernel multiplication and one inverse transform.

A discrete Fourier transform makes the convolution periodic, so each template is first extended at both ends by as many pixels as the largest allowed shift plus several times the largest allowed dispersion, holding its first and last values (zero-filling would drag the ends of the model towards zero, which looks exactly like a continuum slope). Without this the model at 3700 Å would be built partly from the template at 6900 Å.

The velocity search#

The non-linear minimisation needs a starting velocity in the right basin. \(\chi^2\) is evaluated on a grid of \(v_\star\) across velocity_range, step velocity_grid_step (150 km/s), with \(\sigma_\star\), \(A_V\) at their starting values, in spop (the weights are discarded). With coarse_to_fine_grid (default) the grid is scanned in two passes, coarse then fine around the coarse minimum. The best point starts the Levenberg–Marquardt fit.

velocity_range must contain the galaxy’s actual velocity relative to the frame the spectrum is in: for a spectrum still at \(z\) that is roughly \(cz\).

Observed and intrinsic dispersion#

The fitted \(\sigma_\star\) is the broadening applied to the library: the quadrature sum of the stars’ intrinsic dispersion and the difference between the data’s and the library’s line-spread functions, unless those are given (Instrumental resolution), in which case

\[ \sigma_{\star,\rm intr} = \operatorname{sign}(x)\sqrt{|x|}, \qquad x = \sigma_{\rm obs}^2 - \Delta\sigma_{\rm inst}^2 . \]
