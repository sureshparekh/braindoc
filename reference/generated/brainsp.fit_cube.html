fit_cube#

brainsp.fit_cube(cube, redshift, settings=None, *, device=None, gpu_slots='auto', **options)[source]#

Fit one datacube and open the megacube it produces.

Parameters:
cubestr

A datacube FITS file.

redshiftfloat

The galaxy’s redshift (0 for a rest-frame cube).

settingsSettings, dict or str, optional

The physics of the fit.

device{“auto”, “cpu”, “gpu”}, optional

See set_device().

gpu_slots“auto” or int

With more than one, the cube’s spaxels are spread over that many GPU workers (one per GPU by default; see CubePipeline.run()).

**options

Any CubePipeline option (output_dir, wavelength_scale, sn_mask, fit_sn_threshold, wavelength_mask, galactic_ebv …).

Returns:
brainsp.megacube.Megacube
