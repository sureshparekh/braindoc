brainsp.megacube#

Reading a BRAIN megacube, and the emission-line diagnostics drawn from it.

A megacube is the FITS file BRAIN writes for a fitted datacube (<cube>-BRAIN_MEGACUBE.fits): the input cube’s own extensions, then the observed and synthetic spectra on a linear grid (FLXOBS, FLXSYN), the population vectors (PoPVecsL, PoPVecsM), the population maps and fit statistics (PoPBins), and, in a line-fitting mode, the emission-line products in the layout of Riffel et al. 2023 (SOLUTION, FLUX_M, EQW_M …). Megacube opens one and hands out any of its maps by name; brainsp.read_megacube() is the same thing.

Nothing here fits anything or imports JAX or Qt, so it works on a machine with no display.

The diagnostic curves, with their sources:

Kewley et al. 2001     [N II] BPT maximum starburst
Kauffmann et al. 2003  [N II] BPT pure star formation, to its asymptote
                       at log [N II]/Halpha = 0.05
Cid Fernandes et al. 2010   LINER / Seyfert in the [N II] BPT
Kewley et al. 2006     [S II] BPT, star forming and Seyfert / LINER
Cid Fernandes et al. 2011   WHAN

A diagnostic is drawn only where every line it needs has a positive model flux. BRAIN does not yet write line-flux uncertainties, so there is no S/N cut on the lines: a weak line the fit placed at a small positive flux is classified like a strong one.

Bases: object

A BRAIN megacube, read once, with its maps named and grouped.

Parameters:
pathstr

A *-BRAIN_MEGACUBE.fits file.

Attributes:
pathstr
shapetuple of int

(n_y, n_x), the spatial shape.

wavenumpy.ndarray

Rest-frame wavelength of FLXOBS and FLXSYN, in Angstrom.

groupsdict

Map names by group: "Populations", "Kinematics", "Fit quality", "Emission lines", "Diagnostics", "Nebular" (nespop), "Line-spread function" and "Uncertainties" ("<map> error", the 1-sigma error of each map the fit wrote one for).

mapsdict

Every map name, with the extension and plane it is read from.

lineslist of str

The emission lines fitted (empty in spop).

agesnumpy.ndarray

Age of every template, in years, in population-vector order.

statusnumpy.ndarray

1 where a spaxel was fitted, 0 where it was not.

Examples

>>> import brainsp
>>> cube = brainsp.read_megacube("mock_cube-BRAIN_MEGACUBE.fits")
>>> cube.groups["Kinematics"]
['Sigma_star', 'vrot_star', 'Sigma_gas_g0', 'vrot_gas_g0', ...]
>>> velocity = cube.plane("vrot_star")        # 2-D array, NaN where unfitted
>>> obs, model, lines = cube.spectrum(8, 8)
