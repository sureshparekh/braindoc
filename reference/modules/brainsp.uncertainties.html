brainsp.uncertainties#

The uncertainties of everything a fit reports, in every mode.

Two methods, chosen by error_samples:

0 (the default)  FORMAL. Each fit's covariance from the data's errors
                 (fast_cube.parameter_covariance): the inverse Fisher
                 matrix of the weights and the non-linear parameters
                 together, at the solution, on the fit's active set. It is
                 scaled by the fit's chi2 per degree of freedom where that
                 exceeds 1, so a fit worse than its errors allow (template
                 mismatch, underestimated errors) gets wider bars; it is
                 never narrowed below what the errors give. Every reported
                 quantity is then propagated from it to first order.
N > 0            BOOTSTRAP. The whole fit, every stage of it, is repeated
                 on N spectra made from the best model and the fit's own
                 residuals with random signs (the wild bootstrap; Davidson
                 & Flachaire 2008, as Kacharov et al. 2018 for pPXF), and
                 each quantity's error is half the 16-84 per cent range of
                 its N values. It sees what the formal errors cannot: a
                 template that leaves or joins the solution, a parameter
                 that moves to a bound, a spaxel that changes between FC
                 and NC, T_e that is measured in some resamples and not in
                 others, and residuals larger than the errors.

Where each quantity’s formal error comes from:

A_V, Y_V, sigma_*, v_*, the weights and everything built from them (light
and mass fractions, mean ages and metallicities, masses, SFRs, the age
bins):  the population fit, the one whose values are reported. In nespop
that fit has A_V^neb free within its Balmer-decrement error and, in FC,
the measured H-alpha and H-beta with their errors constraining the
ionising photons, so y_neb, Q(H) and tau carry both.
sigma_gas, v_gas:  the joint fit of stars and lines, which measures them.
Line fluxes:  the linear measurement at the fitted kinematics
(fast_cube.line_flux_errors), scaled as above.
A_V^neb, H-alpha/H-beta, T_e, n_e:  from the line-flux errors, through
the same formulae that compute them.

A parameter on a bound of its range has no error (NaN): the fit did not find a minimum there, it stopped at the edge. Flags, counts and the fit’s own statistics (STATUS, MODE, Niter, chi2 …) have none either.

brainsp.uncertainties.chi2_scale(chi2, n_eff, n_free)[source]#

max(1, chi2 / (N_eff - n_free)): the formal covariance’s factor.

brainsp.uncertainties.stage_covariance(ap, aw, fl, isig, lo, hi, T, ld, vs, dk, Df, yd, rwd, rad, ncd, gid, mc_, batch_size, nssp, neb=None, nl=None, lines=None, i0=0, pad_pixels=None)[source]#

One fit’s formal covariance, for every spaxel, in batches.

ap, aw, isig: the fit’s parameters, weights and the pixel weights it ended on (clipped, masked); lo, hi its per-spaxel bounds. The rest are that fit’s constants as fast_cube.fit_queue_fast took them. It returns:

sig    (n, n_par + 1 + n_cols)  1-sigma of every quantity, scaled,
       NaN where it was not free (on a bound, zero weight)
L      (n, n_par + 1 + nssp, K) the population block's square root
       (A_V .. gas, A_V^neb, stellar weights), scaled: its covariance
       is L L^T. K is the largest number of free quantities in it.
g      (n, 3, n_par + 1 + nssp) or None: gradients of S, the nebular
       continuum and the model at pixel i0 (nebular fits)
scale  (n,) the chi2 factor applied

A stage_covariance result of a fit with fewer non-linear parameters (the population fits carry A_V .. v_* only) in the layout of one with npar: the missing ones (the gas kinematics) not free.

brainsp.uncertainties.merge(parts, n)[source]#

stage_covariance results of disjoint spaxel subsets, in one.

brainsp.uncertainties.bound_free(sig, ap, lo, hi)[source]#

NaN for a parameter on a bound (or held), whatever its column gave.

brainsp.uncertainties.propagate(fn, z, L, step=0.0001, chunk=128)[source]#

First-order propagation of z’s covariance L L^T through fn, for every spaxel: sigma_q^2 = sum_k (dq/dz . L_k)^2, the derivative along each column of L by central differences of size step x L_k.

fn(zs, idx) maps a (m, nz) array of z’s, each a perturbed copy of spaxel idx[i], to a dict of (m,) or (m, k) arrays; it is called on all 2 K copies of chunk spaxels at a time.

brainsp.uncertainties.robust_sigma(x, axis=-1)[source]#

Half the 16-84 per cent range: the 1-sigma of a Gaussian, and not pulled by the odd resample that ran to a bound.

brainsp.uncertainties.wild_resamples(fl, af, isl, n_samples, seed=0)[source]#

N spectra per spaxel, (n, N, n_pix): the model plus the residual with a random sign on every pixel the fit used, the data as they are on the rest (masked or clipped pixels stay what they were, so they are masked or clipped again).

brainsp.uncertainties.err_hdu(hdu, sigma, method, extra=None)[source]#

<EXT>_ERR: hdu’s layout and cards, sigma in place of the values.

brainsp.uncertainties.formal_errors(cov_pop, gas_sig, ap, aw, nssp, pop_fn, av_neb=None, lsf_planes=None, gerr=None)[source]#

Every reported quantity’s formal 1-sigma, from the population fit’s covariance (cov_pop, in the layout of the final parameters) and the gas kinematics’ (gas_sig, (n, n_par - 4), NaN where not available).

pop_fn(z, idx) -> {key: values}: the products of a (m, n_par + 1 + nssp) array of [parameters, A_V^neb, stellar weights], keys (‘PoPBins’, card) for the PoPBins planes and ‘PoPVecsL’, ‘PoPVecsM’, ‘PoPVecsMini’ for the vectors. Returns the errors as a dict of the same keys plus ‘KINEMATICS’ (n, n_par), ‘LSFCORR’ {plane: values} and ‘LINES’ (the line weights’ errors, gerr as given).

brainsp.uncertainties.gas_planes(errs, groups)[source]#

The Sigma_gas / vrot_gas planes of PoPBins from KINEMATICS’ errors.

brainsp.uncertainties.nebular_errors(neb_out, cov_pop, p_sig, af, nf, i0, k_hb)[source]#

NEBMAPS’ formal errors: y_neb, Q(H) and the predicted H-beta from the population fit’s covariance (their gradients, cov_pop[“g”]); A_V^neb its posterior there (the decrement’s error as the prior, the photons in FC) or A_V’s where tied; the measured lines’, the decrement’s, T_e’s and n_e’s from the lines’ errors. Flags have none.

brainsp.uncertainties.error_hdus(products, errs, method, yc, xc, npar)[source]#

<EXT>_ERR for every product extension errs covers, aligned with the extension’s own planes (by card for PoPBins and NEBMAPS).
