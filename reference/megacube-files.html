Megacube files#

A datacube’s fit is written to <output_dir>/<stem>-BRAIN_MEGACUBE.fits, with any -RAW removed from the stem. The file keeps the input extensions unchanged and adds the extensions below. brainsp.Megacube reads it by map name (Reading a megacube); any FITS reader can read it directly.

Warning

An unfitted spaxel is 0 in the map extensions, as in the published MaNGA megacubes, and 0 is a plausible value for most quantities. A map value is a measurement only where BADSPXL (and the BadSpaxel plane) is 0. brainsp.Megacube.plane() returns NaN for those spaxels instead.

The compatibility contract#

Six extensions, spelled exactly so, are what existing megacube tools read (Riffel et al. 2023):

Extension

Shape

Content

BaseAgeMetal

(n_base, 2)

column 0 the age in years, column 1 \(Z\) as a mass fraction

PoPBins

(n_map, y, x)

the map stack; plane \(n\) is named by the header card DATA<n>

PoPVecsL

(n_base, y, x)

light fraction of every template, per cent

PoPVecsM

(n_base, y, x)

present-mass fraction of every template, per cent

FLXOBS

(nλ, y, x)

the observed flux in the input units, on a linear wavelength grid

FLXSYN

(nλ, y, x)

the stellar model, on the same grid

FLXOBS and FLXSYN carry CRPIX3, CRVAL3, CD3_3 and WAT3_001, because the existing viewers build their wavelength axis from them. The step is flxobs_step (1 Å by default). This output copy is the only place an observed flux is resampled, and never for the fit; WAVE is written too, and STELLAR and MODEL stay on the cube’s own grid.

Sigma_star, vrot_star

Mage_L and MZ_L follow the published megacubes: summed over the stellar templates but divided by the light of every component, so that featureless light lowers them; divide by the stellar fraction for the mean of the stars alone.

The continuum \(C\) of the equivalent widths is a straight line fitted to the stellar model in each line’s two continuum windows, or a degree-7 polynomial with 3σ clipping where a line has none.

Uncertainties: <EXT>_ERR#

Every map extension has a twin holding the 1σ error of each of its planes, in the same layout and with the same cards: PoPBins_ERR, PoPVecsL_ERR, PoPVecsM_ERR, PoPVecsMini_ERR, KINEMATICS_ERR, NEBMAPS_ERR, LSFCORR_ERR and FLUX_M_ERR. ERRMETH says whether they are formal or the bootstrap’s, and ERRNOTE names what the formal method leaves out. An error plane is NaN where no error is given: unfitted spaxels; flags and counts (Niter, BadSpaxel, MODE); parameters on a bound; with formal errors, the quantities that method cannot describe (age bins, \(x_j\), mass-weighted means, masses, star formation rates, y_neb, tau_Hb); and, with formal errors, every quantity of a spaxel whose \(\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}\) exceeds 2 (Uncertainties).

Two \(\chi^2\) conventions#

CHI2 and PoPBins:ChiSqrt hold \(\chi^2/N_{l,\rm eff}\), \(N_{l,\rm eff}\) being the pixels of non-zero weight that entered \(\chi^2\): what a spectrum’s summary reports on its first line, and what the published megacubes report. CHI2RED holds \(\chi^2/(N_{l,\rm eff} - n_{\rm par})\), with \(n_{\rm par} = 4 + 2n_{\rm groups}\) written to its NPAR card. Each extension states its denominator in a DENOM card, so neither can be assumed silently.

The pre-fitting stages#

Each enabled stage adds its own extension: <flux>_RSP and <uncertainty>_RSP (spatial binning), <flux>_DEGR and <uncertainty>_DEGR (resolution), <flux>_BIN and <uncertainty>_BIN (spectral binning), FLUX_BW (Butterworth filter, with BW_KEPT in its header) and FLUX_DRD (dereddening) (Pre-fitting stages).
