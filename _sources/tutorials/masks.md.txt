# 5. Wavelength masks

A mask leaves wavelength regions out of the fit (weight 0) or counts them
more (weight above 1). A mask file holds the number of regions on the
first line, then `start end weight [label]` for each. Exactly that many
lines are regions; anything after them is notes.

Fitting the stars alone (`spop`) with the emission lines left in drags the
continuum: the dispersion and $\chi^2$ rise. Masking the standard optical
emission-line windows (`brainsp.STANDARD_OPTICAL_MASK`) removes them. In `espop` the lines are
fitted instead and need no mask.

{func}`brainsp.read_mask` returns `(regions, n_skipped)` with each region as
`(start, end, weight, label)`; {func}`brainsp.write_mask` writes the same
layout. A datacube takes one mask for every spaxel
(`CubePipeline(wavelength_mask=...)`); see [](../user_guide/masks.md).
