# 7. A datacube

The mock datacube `mock_cube.fits` is 16 x 16 spaxels laid out like a MaNGA
LOGCUBE (`FLUX`, `IVAR`, `MASK` and `WAVE` extensions, a logarithmic
wavelength axis at 69 km/s per pixel, sky coordinates) and built as a
galaxy: a disc rotating at 220 km/s seen at 55° inclination, an old bulge
with a high velocity dispersion, a ring of young stars, dust and star
formation at 5 spaxels, a LINER-like nucleus, and a signal-to-noise falling
from 58 at the centre to 3 at the edge. The truth is in
`mock_cube.truth.fits`.

## What the pipeline did

{class}`brainsp.CubePipeline` describes the whole run: which cubes, at which
redshift, through which preparation stages, with which fit settings, written
where. Here it:

1. divided each spaxel's wavelengths by $1 + z = 1.02$;
2. computed the signal-to-noise of every spaxel over 4000–6000 Å (rest) and
   wrote masks for S/N > 3, 10 and 20 (`SN_MASKS_3`, ...) and the continuous
   map `SNMAP`;
3. fitted every spaxel with S/N > 3, 172 of the 256, in `espop`;
4. wrote `mock_cube-BRAIN_MEGACUBE.fits`.

The recovered maps match the truth: median offsets of 0.8 km/s in velocity,
0.1 km/s in dispersion and 0.01 mag in $A_V$, with an rms of 6 km/s, 8 km/s
and 0.05 mag, the scatter expected at these signal-to-noise ratios.

The stages a real cube may need, Galactic dereddening, spatial binning,
resolution matching, spectral resampling and filtering, are options of the
same object; [](../user_guide/datacubes.md) describes each.
