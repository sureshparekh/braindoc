# Nebular physics

In `nepop` and `photopop` BRAIN models the continuum the ionised gas emits,
not only its lines, from first principles.

## The ionising photon rate

Each SSP's rate of hydrogen-ionising photons, per unit of its normalisation,
is integrated from the template itself below the Lyman limit:

$$ Q(H)_j = \int_0^{911.76\,\text{Å}} \frac{L_{\lambda,j}\,\lambda}{hc}\, d\lambda . $$

A library whose spectra start redward of 911.76 Å (BasesGM) has none to
integrate, and these modes are refused for it. A library where only some
templates reach the limit is refused too (unless
`allow_partial_ionising_coverage`), because the ionising budget would then be
a property of which templates happen to be long enough.

## The nebular continuum

The emission coefficient of the ionised gas, per unit emission measure, is
the sum of

- **free–free** emission of H$^+$ and He$^+$ (bremsstrahlung), with the
  velocity-averaged Gaunt factor of Sutherland (1998);
- **free–bound** recombination continua of H I (to $n_{\max} = 300$) and He I;
- the **two-photon** continuum of H I $2s \to 1s$ (Nussbaumer & Schmutz 1984)
  and He I,

$$ \gamma_{\rm eff}(\lambda; T_e, n_e) = \gamma_{\rm ff} + \gamma_{\rm fb,HI} + y_{\rm He}\gamma_{\rm fb,HeI} + \gamma_{2q,\rm HI} + y_{\rm He}\gamma_{2q,\rm HeI}, $$

at `gas_temperature` and `gas_density` (or the values measured from the lines,
below), with helium abundance `helium_abundance`. Its strength is
proportional to $Q(H)$ through the Case B recombination coefficient
$\alpha_B$ (Osterbrock & Ferland 2006; Hummer & Storey 1987).

**`nepop`** adds this continuum as one column with a free amplitude; its shape
is physical, its strength fitted. **`photopop`** ties it, and the hydrogen
line strengths, to $Q(H)$ of the stellar populations the fit finds, through
penalty rows that make the hydrogen amplitudes equal the Case B prediction:

$$ w_L = C_{\rm H\beta}\, \frac{j_L}{j_{\rm H\beta}}\, Q_{\rm eff}\, 10^{-0.4 (A_{V,\rm neb} - A_V)\,[q(\lambda_L) - q(\lambda_0)]}, $$

with $A_{V,\rm neb}$ found by a golden-section search on $\chi^2$ over
`nebular_dust_range`. That search is reported with its status (`converged`,
`LOWER_BOUND`, ...) and how much $\chi^2$ varies across the range; read them
before quoting $A_{V,\rm neb}$ ([](../user_guide/modes.md)).

The Balmer jump at 3646 Å is the continuum's distinctive feature; with the
fitting range starting redward of it, the nebular continuum is nearly
degenerate with dust, and BRAIN warns.
