# Theory

What BRAIN computes, equation by equation, with where in the code each piece
lives.

In one paragraph: the observed spectrum, resampled onto a logarithmic
wavelength grid and normalised at $\lambda_0$, is modelled as a non-negative
combination of template spectra (stellar populations, optionally AGN
continua, emission lines and a nebular continuum), each broadened and shifted
by a line-of-sight velocity distribution and attenuated by dust. For any trial
value of the few **non-linear** parameters (extinction, velocities,
dispersions) the best **linear** weights are found exactly by non-negative
least squares; the non-linear parameters are found by a velocity grid search
followed by a bounded Levenberg–Marquardt minimisation of $\chi^2$ over
them. There are no additive or multiplicative polynomials: the shape of the
continuum is the populations' and the dust's.
