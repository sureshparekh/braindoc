# Emission-line diagnostics

With $x = \log([\mathrm{N\,II}]\,6583/\mathrm{H}\alpha)$,
$y = \log([\mathrm{O\,III}]\,5007/\mathrm{H}\beta)$,
$s = \log(([\mathrm{S\,II}]\,6716+6731)/\mathrm{H}\alpha)$ and $W$ the
H$\alpha$ equivalent width (Å, positive for emission), each spaxel is
classified only where every line it needs has a positive model flux.

## BPT [N II]

| Curve | Equation | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| pure star formation | $y = 0.61/(x - 0.05) + 1.30$, $x < 0.05$ | Kauffmann et al. 2003 |
| maximum starburst | $y = 0.61/(x - 0.47) + 1.19$, $x < 0.47$ | Kewley et al. 2001 |
| LINER / Seyfert | $y = 1.01x + 0.48$ | Cid Fernandes et al. 2010 |

Star forming: below Kauffmann. Composite: between Kauffmann and Kewley. Above
Kewley: Seyfert above the Cid Fernandes line, LINER below. (Class values in
the maps: 1 SF, 2 composite, 3 LINER, 4 Seyfert.)

## BPT [S II]

Star forming: $s < 0.32$ and $y < 0.72/(s - 0.32) + 1.30$; otherwise Seyfert
when $y > 1.89s + 0.76$, LINER below (Kewley et al. 2006). (1 SF, 2 LINER,
3 Seyfert.)

## WHAN

Cid Fernandes et al. (2011): retired galaxies $W < 3$ Å; star forming
$x < -0.4$; strong AGN $x \ge -0.4$ and $W > 6$ Å; weak AGN $x \ge -0.4$ and
$3 \le W \le 6$ Å. (1 SF, 2 retired, 3 weak AGN, 4 strong AGN.)

:::{note}
BRAIN does not yet write line-flux uncertainties for a cube, so there is no
signal-to-noise cut on the lines: a faint line the fit placed at a small
positive flux is classified like a strong one. A spaxel's class is no
stronger than its weakest line.
:::

{func}`brainsp.megacube.bpt_nii_class`, {func}`~brainsp.megacube.bpt_sii_class`
and {func}`~brainsp.megacube.whan_class` apply these to any arrays;
{func}`brainsp.plots.diagnostic` draws them.
