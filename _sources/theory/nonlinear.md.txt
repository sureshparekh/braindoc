# The non-linear fit

The non-linear parameters are

$$ \theta = (A_V,\; Y_V,\; \sigma_\star,\; v_\star,\; \sigma_{g_1}, v_{g_1},\; \sigma_{g_2}, v_{g_2}, \ldots), $$

two per kinematic group of emission lines in the line modes. Each is bounded:
`dust_range`, `young_extra_dust_range`, `dispersion_range`,
`velocity_range`, `gas_dispersion_range`, `gas_velocity_range`.

## Variable projection

$\chi^2$ is minimised over $\theta$ with the weights *projected out*: every
evaluation at a trial $\theta$ solves the linear problem exactly
([](linear.md)), so the optimiser sees

$$ \rho(\theta) = W^{1/2}\big(f - A(\theta)\, \hat w(\theta)\big), \qquad \hat w(\theta) = \arg\min_{w\ge0} \chi^2(\theta, w), $$

a residual of a handful of parameters instead of a hundred.

## Projected Levenberg–Marquardt

From the velocity-search starting point ([](kinematics.md)), each iteration
solves

$$ (J^{\rm T}J + \mu\, \mathrm{diag}(s)^2)\, \delta = -J^{\rm T}\rho $$

with $J = \partial\rho/\partial\theta$, $s$ the running maximum of the
columns' norms (Moré's scaling), and clips $\theta + \delta$ into the bounds.
A step is accepted if it lowers $\chi^2$; the damping $\mu$ falls by 3 after a
good step (gain ratio > 0.75) and rises by 3 after a poor or rejected one.
It stops when the relative change in $\chi^2$ is below `fit_tolerance` and
the step is below `fit_tolerance` of the bounds' width (after at least 4
iterations), when $\chi^2$ has stagnated over five accepted steps, after ten
consecutive rejections, or at `max_iterations` (200). A spaxel that stops at
`max_iterations` is reported as not converged.

## The Jacobian

For a single spectrum $J$ is estimated by finite differences (forward, or
central with `central_differences`), re-broadening the templates only for the
two parameters that change the broadening ($\sigma_\star$, $v_\star$).

A datacube's fast fitter (`cube_fitter: fast`, the default) computes $J$
exactly, by the Golub–Pereyra variable-projection formula. With $P$ the
passive (non-zero) set of the weights and $G = A_P^{\rm T}A_P$,

$$ \frac{\partial\rho}{\partial\theta_i} = -(I - \Pi_P)\,\frac{\partial A}{\partial\theta_i}\hat w \;-\; A_P G^{-1}\Big[\Big(\frac{\partial A}{\partial\theta_i}\Big)^{\rm T}\rho\Big]_P , $$

with $\Pi_P = A_P G^{-1}A_P^{\rm T}$ and $\partial A/\partial\theta_i$ from the
analytic derivatives of the broadening kernel and the attenuation. Measured on
64 MaNGA spaxels it gives the same fit as finite differences (largest change
in any fitted value $5\times10^{-4}$ km/s) 2.4–3.9 times faster.
