# Mock data

{mod}`brainsp.mock` builds spectra and a datacube whose right answer is
known, for the tutorials, the self-test, and for testing an analysis before
trusting it on real data.

```python
folder = brainsp.mock.write_examples()          # ~/.brainsp/examples
```

```bash
brainsp examples [FOLDER]
```

Each spectrum has a `.truth.json` beside it, the cube a `.truth.fits`.

## The forward model

Written out in {mod}`brainsp.mock`, independently of the fitter:

1. **Population**: chosen BasesGM templates, each normalised to 1 at 5500 Å,
   weighted by light fraction.
2. **Dust**: multiplied by $10^{-0.4 A_V [q(\lambda) - q(5500)]}$ with the CCM
   law (a NumPy copy, agreeing with BRAIN's to $4\times10^{-7}$).
3. **Kinematics**: a Gaussian line-of-sight velocity distribution on a
   logarithmic grid of 6.9 km/s per pixel, then shifted by $v_\star$.
4. **Emission lines**: Gaussians at BRAIN's vacuum rest wavelengths, the
   tied doublets at their atomic ratios, shifted and broadened by the gas
   kinematics.
5. **Redshift** ($\lambda \times (1+z)$, flux density not rescaled), then
   **Galactic dust** at observed wavelengths.
6. **Noise**: Gaussian, S/N given at 5500 Å, scaling as $\sqrt{F}$; 0.2 per
   cent of pixels flagged bad.

The model is built on a grid ten times finer than the output so that
sampling it does not itself broaden the lines (sampling from a 69 km/s grid
adds about 28 km/s in quadrature).

## The cube

A disc rotating at 220 km/s (turnover 2.5 spaxels) seen at 55° and position
angle 30°; a bulge with $\sigma_\star$ from 200 km/s at the centre to 60 km/s;
an old population dominating the centre, an intermediate one the disc, a
young one in a ring at 5 spaxels; dust peaking in that ring; H II-region
emission in the ring and LINER-like emission in the nucleus; S/N from 58 at
the centre to 3 at the edge; spaxels outside a 7.4-spaxel radius empty
(`MASK` bit 10 set, `IVAR` 0). Layout: `FLUX` (1e-17 erg/s/cm²/Å/spaxel),
`IVAR`, `MASK`, `WAVE`, logarithmic at $\Delta\log_{10}\lambda = 10^{-4}$,
celestial WCS with 0.5″ spaxels.

## Your own

```python
wave, flux, err, flag, truth = brainsp.mock.make_spectrum(
    population=[(1e9, 0.019, 0.6), (1e10, 0.019, 0.4)],
    av=0.3, v=120, sigma=150, sn=50, lines="liner", halpha=3.0,
    redshift=0.05, seed=7)
brainsp.mock.write_spectrum("my_mock.spec", wave, flux, err, flag, truth)
brainsp.mock.write_cube("my_cube.fits", n=24, redshift=0.01)
```

See {func}`brainsp.mock.make_spectrum` for every option.
