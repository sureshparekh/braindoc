# AGN continuum

An active nucleus adds a featureless continuum that no stellar population
reproduces. Fitted without it, its blue light is taken by spurious young
populations and its red light by extra dust ([tutorial 3](../tutorials/agn.md)).

```python
brainsp.fit_spectrum("seyfert.spec", include_power_law=True)
```

`include_power_law: true` (interface: *Include AGN continuum templates (7)*)
adds seven templates to whichever library is used:

| Template | What it is |
|---|---|
| `FC0.50`, `FC1.00`, `FC1.50`, `FC2.00` | power laws $F_\nu \propto \nu^{-\alpha}$, $\alpha$ = 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 |
| `AGN_Cloudy_T1.0e5`, `T1.5e5`, `T2.0e5` | Cloudy's AGN incident continuum with a big-bump temperature of $1$, $1.5$ and $2\times10^5$ K, $\alpha_{ox} = -1.4$, $\alpha_{uv} = -0.5$, $\alpha_x = -1$ |

All are normalised at 5268 Å and tabulated over 1000–25 000 Å. In the optical
the Cloudy continua are power laws of slope 0.74, 0.64 and 0.59 to within
1.4 per cent, so they and the FC series overlap: **only the summed AGN light
is a measurement**, not its split between templates. A library's own power
law (BasesGM's `PowerOpt_150`, identical to `FC1.50`) is left out when these
are added.

## In the results

- The population table lists each AGN template with its light fraction
  `x_j` and zero mass: a power law has no stars.
- `FitResult.agn_fraction` is the summed AGN light at the normalisation
  wavelength, in per cent; the summary has `x_AGN (%)`.
- The megacube has an `FC<α>` plane per power law and an `AGN_light` plane.

The AGN templates are in `brainsp/data/libraries/AGN` with a
`CATALOGUE.txt` whose `KIND` is `powerlaw`; they are never offered as a
library on their own.
