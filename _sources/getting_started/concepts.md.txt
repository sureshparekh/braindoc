# Concepts

The words the rest of the documentation uses, in one place.

**Spectrum**
: A one-dimensional galaxy spectrum: wavelength (Å), flux and, ideally, an
  error and a bad-pixel flag per pixel. A plain text file. See
  [](../user_guide/inputs.md).

**Datacube**
: An integral-field (IFU) observation: a spectrum at every spaxel of a
  2-D field, in a FITS file (MaNGA, MUSE, CALIFA, ...).

**Template library** (or *base*)
: A set of **simple stellar population** (SSP) spectra: the light of a
  population of stars of one age and one metallicity, all born at once. BRAIN
  ships *BasesGM* (21 ages from 1 Myr to 12.6 Gyr, 4 metallicities) and
  *M13* (Maraston 2013, 12 ages, 4 metallicities, into the infrared); more
  can be added (see [](../user_guide/libraries.md)).

**Fit**
: Finding the non-negative mixture of templates, reddened by dust and
  broadened and shifted by the stars' motions, that reproduces the spectrum
  pixel by pixel ("full spectral fitting"); see [](../theory/index.md).

**Light fraction** $x_j$
: The share of the light at the **normalisation wavelength** (5500 Å by
  default) that template $j$ contributes. Light fractions are not mass
  fractions: a young population is far brighter per unit mass.

**Mass fraction** $\mu_j$
: The share of the stellar mass. *Initial* mass (all ever formed) and
  *present* mass (still in stars and remnants) differ by the mass returned
  to the gas.

**Kinematics**
: The line-of-sight velocity $v_\star$ and velocity dispersion
  $\sigma_\star$ of the stars, and the same for each group of emission lines.

**Extinction** $A_V$
: Magnitudes of dust absorption in the V band, with a wavelength dependence
  set by an **extinction law** (CCM by default).

**Nebular mode**
: How much of the ionised gas is modelled: `spop` (stars only), `espop`
  (emission lines too), `nepop` (and a nebular continuum), `photopop` (and
  the gas tied to the ionising photons of the young stars). See
  [](../user_guide/modes.md).

**Corrections**
: Things undone *before* the fit: the redshift, and the Milky Way's
  reddening (Galactic $E(B-V)$). The galaxy's own dust is not a correction; it
  is fitted.

**Mask**
: Wavelength regions left out of the fit (weight 0) or counted more
  (weight > 1). For a datacube a **signal-to-noise mask** also chooses which
  spaxels are fitted.

**Megacube**
: The FITS file a datacube fit writes: the input cube, plus the observed and
  model spectra, the population vectors, maps of every fitted and derived
  quantity, and the emission-line products. See [](../user_guide/outputs.md).

**Device**
: Where JAX runs the fit: `cpu`, `gpu`, or `auto`. Chosen once per Python
  process, before the first fit.

**Settings**
: Every choice BRAIN makes, 68 of them, each with a default: a
  {class}`~brainsp.Settings` object, a YAML file, or the controls of the
  interface. See [](../reference/settings.md).
