# Quick start: the desktop interface

```bash
brainsp examples        # mock data to ~/.brainsp/examples, which the
                        # interface opens with
brainsp gui
```

```{image} ../_static/gui/light_spectrum_empty.png
:alt: BRAIN on start-up, Spectrum workspace, step 1.
:class: screenshot
```

The window has three workspaces, chosen at the top left:

**Spectrum**
: fit one spectrum, or a table of them.

**Datacube**
: prepare and fit a datacube, and watch it being fitted.

**Results**
: open a fitted megacube and read its maps, spectra and diagrams.

Under them, each workspace is a row of numbered **steps**, done in order.
Each step shows its state on one line: a green check when it is ready, a
red **!** when it needs input. The step's controls are on the left; the data
being worked on (the spectrum, the cube) are on the right throughout. Every
step and every panel is tagged **Required** or **Optional**: the optional
ones have sound defaults and can be left alone.

## Fit a spectrum in five steps

1. **Spectrum** (required): pick `mock_old_population.spec` from the list.
   It is drawn on the right.
2. **Corrections** (optional): the redshift and the Milky Way's
   reddening to remove before fitting. This mock is already in the rest
   frame, so leave it.
3. **Masks** (optional): drag across the spectrum to leave a region out of
   the fit, or load a `.sm` mask file.
4. **Model** (required): the template library, the ages and metallicities,
   the nebular mode, dust and kinematics. The defaults fit this mock.
5. **Fit**: check the summary and press **Fit spectrum** (top right).

The fit appears over the data, with the stellar and emission-line
components, and its headline numbers (A_V, sigma, v0, chi2, adev) in the
bar at the bottom.

## Fit a datacube in six steps

Choose **Datacube**, then:

1. **Cubes**: *Open datacube…* and pick `cube/mock_cube.fits`. Its flux
   map appears on the right; click a spaxel to see its spectrum.
2. **Corrections**: the redshift, **0.02** for this mock (required).
3. **Preparation** (optional): binning, resolution, filtering. Skip.
4. **Masks**: click S/N **3**: the map greys out the spaxels it would leave
   out. Optionally drag on the spaxel spectrum to mask wavelengths.
5. **Model**: the same settings as for spectra; choose **espop** and, in
   step 1, the wavelength scale **vacuum**.
6. **Run**: *Run the pipeline*. Each spaxel lights up in blue on the map as
   it is fitted; *View results* opens the megacube when it is done.

[](../gui/index.md) goes through every control of every step, in both themes.
