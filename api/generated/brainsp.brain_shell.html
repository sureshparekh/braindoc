brainsp.brain_shell#

BRAIN GUI: the workspace shell.

Every workspace (a spectrum, a datacube) is a step bar over two columns:

step bar: 1 Spectrum > 2 Corrections > 3 Masks > 4 Model > 5 Fit
the current step's controls | the stage

The bar is the workflow in the order it is done. Each step shows an icon, its name and one line of state, and whether it is ready, so the state of a whole fit can be read across the top without opening anything. Only the current step’s controls are on screen, in a column as wide as the window allows; the stage is the thing being worked on (a spectrum and its fit, a cube and its maps) and stays put whichever step is open.

A step is numbered because the steps are a sequence: corrections are applied before the fit, and the model is chosen before it runs. A step that is off (an optional stage nobody switched on) is drawn quietly rather than hidden, because a stage that disappears cannot be read to find out what it would have done.
