About#

BRAIN is a full spectral fitting code for single spectra, large samples of spectra and IFU datacubes. It recovers the stellar populations, the dust, the stellar and gas kinematics and the emission lines of a source, writes a 1σ error for every quantity it measures, and runs on a laptop CPU or on one or several GPUs. It is written in JAX and is distributed as the Python package brainsp, with a desktop interface and a command line.

BRAIN is developed at the Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS), Porto Alegre, Brazil, in the SAFFARY group.
