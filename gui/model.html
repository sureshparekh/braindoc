The model panel#

The Model step decides what every spectrum, or every spaxel, is fitted with. The Spectrum and the Datacube workspaces show the same panel, so the two are fitted with the same physics unless you change it. Each control below is listed with the setting it writes, which is its name in Python, in a settings file and on the command line (Settings).

Nebular treatment#

Required. How much of the light the fit attributes to ionised gas (The three fitting modes).

Control

Setting

Values

spop / espop / nespop

nebular_mode

spop (default): stars only. espop: and emission lines. nespop: and the nebular continuum, with H\(\alpha\) and H\(\beta\) tied to the young stars’ ionising photons

The caption under the buttons describes the chosen mode, and a second caption says whether the selected library can run nespop. Choosing nespop selects a library that can run it and greys out those that cannot; a library that reaches the Lyman limit but has not been validated for nespop (M13) stays selectable, with a warning.

espop and nespop use the Wavelength scale answered in step 1 of either workspace, and do not run without it.

Stellar templates#

Required. The simple stellar populations the fit mixes.

Control

Setting

What it does

Library

base_folder

the template library: BasesGM (default), M13, and any library you have added (Template libraries)

Age

ages

the youngest and oldest ages used; every template of the library in between is included

Metallicity

metallicities

the lowest and highest metallicity used

Include AGN continuum templates (7)

include_power_law

adds four power laws (\(F_\nu \propto \nu^{-\alpha}\), \(\alpha\) = 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2) and three Cloudy AGN continua (\(T\) = 1, 1.5, 2 × 10⁵ K)

The line under the sliders counts the templates the choice gives (for example 21 ages × 4 metallicities → 84 templates).

Tip

Only the summed light of the AGN templates, \(x_{\rm AGN}\), is a measurement. A featureless continuum can stand in for dust, so include them where an AGN is expected and leave them out otherwise (An active nucleus).

Fitting range#

Optional. Which wavelengths are fitted.

Control

Setting

What it does

range slider, From / to

wavelength_min, wavelength_max

the fitted range, 3700 to 6900 Å by default; drag the slider or type the limits

Normalised at λ₀

normalisation_wavelength

where the templates and the data are normalised, so the wavelength the light fractions refer to

The range can only cover wavelengths that both the data and the library have; the caption says which of the two sets each limit. A new spectrum keeps the range if it still fits, and otherwise takes all of its own. \(\lambda_0\) follows the range (5500 Å when the range holds it, the middle of the range otherwise) until you type a value. Choose a stretch of clean continuum: for a near-infrared cube, for example, the S/N window.

Note

The lower limit of \(-1\) is deliberate. A negative \(A_V\) lets the fit say that the templates are bluer than the data need, rather than pinning \(A_V\) at zero and hiding the mismatch in the populations (Dust).

Kinematics#

Optional. The ranges the stellar line-of-sight velocity distribution may take.

Control

Setting

Default

Stellar velocity

velocity_range

−2000 to 5000 km s⁻¹

Velocity dispersion

dispersion_range

10 to 500 km s⁻¹

The velocity is measured in the frame the spectrum is in after the corrections, so for a de-redshifted spectrum it is the velocity relative to the redshift you gave. The gas kinematics have ranges of their own (gas_velocity_range, gas_dispersion_range), set in Python or a settings file.

Fit quality#

Optional.

Control

Setting

What it does

Smoothness

smoothness

regularisation of the star formation history; 0 (default) is none, higher values give smoother histories

Error samples

error_samples

0 (default): formal uncertainties, computed from the fit itself at no cost. N > 0: the bootstrap, the whole fit repeated N times (40 to 100 is typical); the only uncertainties of age bins and mass-weighted means (Uncertainties)

Reject outlier pixels

reject_outliers

one clip-and-refit pass at 3σ (on by default), against cosmic rays and bad pixels the flags missed

Settings the panel does not show#

Every other setting keeps its default here. To change one, export the run as a Python file and edit it (Exporting maps and runs), or fit from Python or the command line. The ones most often needed are:

Setting

What it does

instrumental_resolution

your spectrograph’s resolution, so that \(\sigma_\star\) and \(\sigma_{\rm gas}\) are intrinsic

library_resolution

the template library’s resolution, when its files do not state it

line_groups, gas_velocity_range, gas_dispersion_range

which lines share their kinematics, and the ranges those may take

line_components, max_line_components

several Gaussian components per line, for outflows

gas_temperature, gas_density, measure_gas_conditions

the gas conditions of nespop

young_extra_dust_range

the range of the young populations’ extra extinction \(Y_V\)
